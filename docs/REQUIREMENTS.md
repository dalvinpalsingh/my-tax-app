# Requirements

My Tax App is a Malaysian personal finance and tax planning companion for an individual taxpayer with sole-proprietor business activities. Complete v1 covers all the user's income, business expenses and personal tax reliefs. One internal user manages multiple activities, registrations and income sources. An optional spouse profile supports household simulations. Future taxpayers and entity types need explicit ownership and effective dates.

## Financial records

- Record MYR and USD accounts, opening balances, manual entries and imported history. Prioritize available records from 2024 onward. Keep account purpose and ownership effective-dated. Classify transactions by purpose and evidence, including when personal funds pay business expenses.
- Share transactions across account activity, budgets, business reports and tax workpapers. Support splits, partial matches, refunds, fees, reversals, transfers and owner drawings. Internal transfers and deposit principal movements must not inflate income or expenses.
- Track independent contracting, unit trust commissions and other income. Manage historical and new invoices with client-specific templates, numbering, payment terms, PDF output, email correspondence and recurring templates. Support duplication, partial and multi-currency settlements, advances, overpayments, credits and cancellation history.
- Track commission statement periods, gross amounts, adjustments and clawbacks separately from net bank receipts. Link CP58 and self-billed e-Invoice evidence when available. Retain configurable e-Invoice compliance fields. Direct MyInvois integration is deferred.
- Track business, personal, mixed-use, capital and non-deductible expenses, including staff and subcontractor payments. Keep asset cost and allocation auditable. Full payroll compliance is deferred.
- Track USD invoices, settlement, holdings, actual conversions and fees. Track deposit purpose, placement, maturity, renewal, principal and interest separately.

## Tax planning and reporting

- Target Borang B estimates and evidence-backed workpapers. Support YA 2024 onward initially and earlier years wherever validated rules are available. Track reliefs, donations, allowances, losses, credits and payments. Suggest classifications with explanations, review flags and reasoned overrides.
- Support individual, spouse and dependant records and eligible separate and joint assessment comparisons. Prevent duplicate claims. Retain actual submitted returns as immutable snapshots and show differences against recalculated estimates.
- Show the selected YA's tax position first, followed by income, account balances, reserves and review tasks. Include filters for activity, account and income source, and comparisons across years.
- Project income from historical patterns with adjustable assumptions. Save baseline and what-if scenarios without modifying actual records. Track projected liability, payments, earmarked funds and funding gaps independently.
- Support physical reserves and virtual envelopes, configurable by YA. Include complete personal budgeting in v1 with monthly and annual budgets, recurring spending, targets, rollover, savings goals and trends. Keep Daswandh allocations, actual giving and eligible donations distinct. Use a configurable contribution basis. The app proposes allocations for manual reconciliation.
- Provide accountant-ready business statements, LHDN-oriented worksheets, filing comparisons, and PDF/Excel exports. Provide in-app and email reminders for important dates.

## Evidence and delivery

Use manual entry and CSV/XLSX import from the first financial milestone. Support in-app receipt uploads and private Google Drive links. Preserve historical evidence from spreadsheets, PDFs and bank records. Full Drive sync and OCR follow v1. Deliver a responsive, installable, online-first app. Direct bank integration and offline financial editing are outside v1.
